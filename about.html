About Al-Razi Dental

Hospital-grade surgical precision, advanced orthodontics, and restorative dental excellence in Gulberg III, Lahore.

Academic Clinical Standards

Pioneering Maxillofacial & Dental Healthcare in Lahore

Al-Razi Dental & Maxillofacial Care was established by Prof. Dr. Tariq Mahmood to bridge the gap between private dental clinics and tertiary hospital surgical standards. Located at Al-Hafeez Heights in Gulberg III, our practice combines academic expertise with state-of-the-art digital dental technologies.

We adhere strictly to World Health Organization (WHO) cross-infection control guidelines. Every instrument undergoes multi-stage ultrasonic cleaning and Class-B vacuum autoclaving before single-patient use.
