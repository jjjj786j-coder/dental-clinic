Privacy Policy & Confidentiality Statement

At Al-Razi Dental & Maxillofacial Care, we prioritize the strict confidentiality and protection of our patients' personal and medical records in accordance with international healthcare privacy standards and the Pakistan Medical & Dental Council (PMDC) regulations.

  • Medical Data Handling: All clinical records, digital X-rays, 3D CBCT scans, and treatment histories are stored securely on encrypted local servers accessible only by authorized attending specialists.
  • Information Collection: Contact information provided via online booking forms or WhatsApp is used exclusively for appointment scheduling, clinical follow-ups, and prescription reminders. We do not share, sell, or disclose patient data to third-party advertisers.
  • Patient Rights: Patients have full rights to request copies of their diagnostic records, dental X-rays, and treatment receipts at any time during clinical hours.

Terms of Clinical Care & Cancellation Policy

By booking an appointment or receiving dental treatment at Al-Razi Dental & Maxillofacial Care, patients agree to the following operational policies:

  • Punctuality & Arrival: Please arrive 10 minutes prior to your scheduled consultation time to complete health intake questionnaires and sterilization prep.
  • Cancellations & Rescheduling: If you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify our reception desk at least 4 hours in advance via phone (+92 42 35789012) or WhatsApp (+92 301 8472915).
  • Sterilization Protocols: To maintain strict WHO sterilization standards, patient companions may be requested to wait in our designated reception lounge during surgical procedures.
  • Payment & Invoicing: Detailed itemized invoices are issued for all procedures. We accept Cash, Bank Wire Transfers, Debit/Credit Cards, and major insurance coverage where pre-approved.
