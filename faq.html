Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions regarding dental procedures, insurance, and clinic policies.

We recommend visiting Lumina Dental every 6 months for a routine examination and professional cleaning. Regular visits help detect early signs of tooth decay, gum disease, and other oral health issues before they require major treatment.
We accept most major PPO dental insurance providers, including Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, and Guardian. Our administrative team will happily process claims on your behalf and help you maximize your insurance benefits.
If you experience severe pain, a knocked-out tooth, a cracked tooth, or uncontrolled bleeding, call our emergency hotline at (555) 234-5678 immediately. We reserve dedicated daily appointment slots specifically for emergency patients.
Yes! Professional teeth whitening under dental supervision is thoroughly tested and completely safe for your enamel. Our treatments include desensitizing agents to ensure minimal to no post-treatment sensitivity.
Clear aligners are nearly invisible, removable for eating and brushing, and generally more comfortable than metal brackets. However, traditional braces may still be recommended for complex structural adjustments. Schedule a consultation to discover which option fits your smile best.
Your comfort is our top priority. We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral sedation options, along with noise-canceling headphones and a calm, supportive clinical staff to make your visit completely stress-free.
