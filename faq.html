Invisalign® aligners are custom-molded, transparent plastic trays designed to align teeth virtually invisibly without metal wires or brackets.

  • Aesthetics: Nearly invisible during conversations and meetings.
  • Convenience: Completely removable for eating, brushing, and flossing.
  • Comfort: No sharp metal brackets to cause cheek or gum abrasions.
  • Treatment Duration: Typically 30% faster than traditional braces with 3D digital progress tracking.

Patient safety is our single non-negotiable priority. We adhere to Class-B European Autoclave Sterilization standards recommended by the World Health Organization (WHO).

  • All re-usable surgical instruments undergo ultrasonic bath cleaning followed by 134°C high-pressure steam sterilization.
  • Instruments are sealed in vacuum pouches and opened strictly in front of the patient.
  • Single-use disposable suction tips, needles, gloves, and patient drapes are discarded after every treatment session.

Dental implants replace missing tooth roots with biocompatible medical-grade titanium posts anchored into the jawbone.

The surgical placement is performed under local anesthesia and is completely painless. Initial tissue healing takes 3 to 5 days, after which osseointegration (bone bonding) occurs over 8 to 12 weeks before fixing the custom porcelain crown.
